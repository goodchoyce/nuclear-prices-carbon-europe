# Glossary

Short definitions for terms used in this project. Where a term has multiple common meanings, the meaning used here is stated explicitly.

---

**Capacity (GW).** Maximum continuous output a power station can deliver. Not the same as generation. A high-capacity coal plant running rarely will have low generation.

**Generation (TWh).** Electrical energy produced over a period. Used as the primary measure of "what the grid actually did".

**Generation share (%).** A fuel's generation divided by total domestic generation. Does not account for imports or exports.

**Demand (TWh).** Electricity consumed in the country, including imports and net of exports. Differs from domestic generation when there is significant trade.

**Net imports (TWh).** Electricity imported minus electricity exported. Positive means a net importer over the period.

**Power-sector carbon intensity (gCO₂/kWh).** CO₂ emissions from electricity generation divided by electricity generated. Generation-based unless stated otherwise.

**Generation-based carbon intensity.** Calculated from generation inside the country. Does not include the carbon content of imported electricity.

**Consumption-based carbon intensity.** Calculated from electricity consumed in the country, adjusting for imports and exports. Not used in this project's primary tables; planned cross-check via Electricity Maps.

**Low-carbon generation.** Nuclear, hydro, wind, solar, bioenergy and other renewables. Bioenergy is treated as low-carbon at the power-sector boundary; lifecycle emissions are not zero.

**Fossil generation.** Coal, gas and other fossil sources.

**Aggregate fuel (Ember).** Pre-rolled groupings such as "Renewables", "Fossil", "Wind and Solar". Overlaps with individual fuel rows and must not be summed alongside them.

**Fuel (Ember).** Individual fuels: Nuclear, Hydro, Wind, Solar, Bioenergy, Other Renewables, Gas, Coal, Other Fossil. Used for per-fuel sums in this project.

**Band DC (Eurostat household prices).** Annual consumption between 2,500 kWh and 4,999 kWh. Used as the medium-household reference band.

**All taxes and levies included (Eurostat).** The retail price including VAT and energy-related levies. The most consumer-facing measure; not the same as the wholesale price or the cost to generate.

**EUR per kWh.** Currency unit used for cross-country price comparisons. Converts non-EUR currencies at Eurostat's exchange rates.

**Bidding zone (ENTSO-E).** A geographic area with a single day-ahead market price. Often the country, but not always; for example, historically Germany–Luxembourg shared a zone.

**Latest full year.** 2024 in this project. 2025 is treated as preliminary.

**Association vs causation.** An association is a statistical relationship between variables. A causal claim requires a mechanism and the ability to rule out confounders. This project reports associations only.

**Regional carbon intensity.** Carbon intensity reported below national level. In this project, the exact regional unit will depend on the source data and may be county, ITL/NUTS region or electricity grid region.

**ITL/NUTS region.** A statistical region used for comparing areas below country level. The UK now uses ITL, which replaced the former UK NUTS classification after Brexit.

**County boundary.** An administrative or geographic boundary used for mapping. County boundaries may be familiar to UK audiences but may not match electricity-system regions.

**Grid region.** A region used by an electricity-system dataset or network operator. It may reflect how electricity data is collected or modelled rather than standard administrative geography.

**Spatial join.** A data-processing step that attaches data values to map boundaries, usually by matching region names, codes or geographic shapes.
